## 2. How it works

Three roles. Be any of them, any day. Most people end up being all three.

### Pilot

*You've got the scooty. Someone's got the time.*

Say where you're going and when: "around 4pm, Arambol, 1 seat free." A hitcher claims it. You ride, you chat (or don't), you both earn kaju. Pilots earn more — you brought the wheels.

### Hitcher

*No scooty? No problem. Say the word.*

Post "around 4pm I want to go to the pharmacy," and the village finds you a seat. Need one right now? Send a ride-now alert. Fewer kaju, same goodwill. One helmet, one person, twenty minutes. That's it.

### Floater

*The hero of the story. Also: not doing much.*

Floating is for the day you have nowhere to be. Turn on live location in Telegram, tell the bot "I'm around, ping me," and go lie on the sand. When someone needs a ride, you're the nearest answer. You're not giving up your plans — you're giving the village your lack of them. Aimlessness, donated. That's why floaters earn the most kaju. Susegad, but make it useful.

## 4. Why early?

A ride only happens when two plans cross. If you tell the bot at breakfast that you're off to Ashwem at four, the bot has all day to find your match — and to nudge the one person who was quietly hoping someone would say exactly that. A ride-now alert works too, but it only has minutes, and it only sees who's floating nearby. Early plans turn the village into a timetable. Late ones turn it into luck.

## 9. FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes. Kaju is pretend money. Rides are favours between neighbours. The prizes are real, but you can't buy them — only earn them.

**Is it a taxi?**
No. Nobody's working. It's one villager going somewhere and another one tagging along. If you want a taxi, the shuttle group is that way.

**Do I need a scooty?**
Not at all. Hitchers earn kaju too, and half the village is on foot. You just need a Telegram account and somewhere to be.

**What if I cancel?**
Life happens. Cancel early and nothing happens. Cancel last-minute and you lose 5 kaju — a tiny nudge, because someone was waiting by the gate. No hard feelings.

**Is my location tracked all the time?**
Only while you're floating, and only because you switched Telegram live location on. Switch it off and you vanish from the map. The bot only ever sees village members — everyone's verified through the Edge City Telegram. Wear a helmet. Ride with people from here. That's the whole safety talk.

## 11. Beta (20 Oct launch)
Stamp: **Beta · an experiment**. Pills saying "beta" sit by the eyebrow, the "Three ways to play" heading, the app's
logo and the footer; the 🧪 **Beta · feedback** button floats in the corner of every page.

> **This is a beta, and an experiment. Let's test it out together.**
> Pillion is our experiment in sharing rides around the village. Most of us have a back seat that rides empty, or a
> car with three spare seats, and most of us have stood at Riva wondering how to get to the Circle. We think the
> village can fix that for itself, if we plan a little.
>
> **That's why we reward planning.** We want finding a ride to be predictable, not a last-minute, half-hearted
> scramble. When you say at breakfast that you're going to Arambol at four, someone can plan around you. So early
> plans earn the most kaju, and a ride-now alert earns a little.
>
> Some things will break. Please tell us when they do, and tell us what you wish it did.

"Why tell us early?" adds: *We'd rather value your planning than your speed. A village where people say early where
they're going is one where a ride is something you can count on, not something you hope for.*
